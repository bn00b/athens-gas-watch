How this data works ▼

Live, pump-by-pump pricing lives behind GasBuddy's and AAA's own apps and isn't available as an open feed, so the 25 stations below are a modeled illustration, not a live scrape. Brands and roads reflect real Athens-area corridors — Atlanta Hwy, Lexington Rd, Epps Bridge Pkwy, Baxter St, Prince Ave — plus the surrounding towns of Bogart, Watkinsville, Winterville, Bishop, Nicholson, Hull, and Statham.

Per-station regular prices are built around AAA's verified Georgia average for today (shown in the summary above), spread using typical local competitive patterns: warehouse-club and grocery fuel centers cheapest, single-brand stations in smaller towns highest. Mid-grade, premium, and diesel apply that day's real AAA Georgia grade ratios to each station's regular price. A GitHub Actions job re-fetches AAA's state average daily and re-anchors these figures to it — the station list and relative spread stay modeled, but the price level tracks the real market. For an exact price at a specific pump right now, check the GasBuddy or AAA app.

The Credit / Cash toggle applies a flat 10¢/gal discount for cash, a common posted-price practice at Georgia convenience stores. AAA's average itself doesn't split by payment method (it's effectively the credit/posted price), and the real discount varies by station — some retailers, including most warehouse clubs, charge one price regardless of how you pay.

Source: AAA Gas Prices, Georgia state average (gasprices.aaa.com), refreshed automatically once per day.
